## Commercial-clean subset (MIT + CC0)

If NodeAva moves to a paid distribution, these three avatars are the
no-strings-attached subset that can ship without license concerns:

- `rpm-male.glb` (MIT) — photoreal male
- `rpm-female.glb` (MIT) — photoreal female
- `mpfb.glb` (CC0) — MakeHuman baseline

That's a minimal commercial-clean gallery covering both genders + a CC0
baseline. The other five (`default-avatar`, `rpm-male-casual`, `rpm-male-smart`,
`avatarsdk-male`, `avaturn`) are non-commercial or developer-use and
bundled here under educational/workshop terms.

## Stylized variety — still open

Anime / cartoon / robot characters proved harder than expected: the
Polygonal Mind 100Avatars CC0 collection (Polybot robot, Froggy frog) and
the previous `vroid.glb` were attempted but pulled from the gallery. Cartoon
characters lack the eye bones TalkingHead's animation system requires; vroid
worked structurally but its default outfit wasn't appropriate for the
conference. Filed as Blender follow-up work (task #148).
