What you're testing: Whisper (the base.en model) running in a Docker container with Vulkan or CUDA GPU acceleration. This is the perception layer — the part that hears speech and converts it to text. What you'll see: the time Whisper took vs the length of your recording. The ratio is RTF (real-time factor) — under 1.0 means it transcribed faster than real-time. On a workshop GPU you'll typically see 0.05-0.20 RTF, i.e. 5-20x faster than speech. Endpointing — knowing when the user is done talking — is a separate problem; we cover that in Lab 6.
